# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-11:** Version 11 is merged on `main` and published. It shapes the mountains from everywhere (the walkable slopes and the skyline: peaks, saddles, ribs and gullies, outcrops and hardy trees on the lower slopes, done once everything is placed so every save keeps its indices); makes every tower of every keep a place you can go into (Logres's gate drums, Ériu's gate drums and lookouts, Thule's log towers that burn, fall in and are rebuilt) with fighting on ladders and at hatches; keeps feet from sliding (beasts vault over planted legs, a foot lock holds planted feet through shoves and turns, feet stand on each stair tread); lets bodies push boats and shove them off the shore, and keeps tracks with the world; and brings the callings nearer the aim (a warrior's pursuit and shield wall against kiting, frost that holds, half-piercing bodkins, holy light that dazzles, better skilled bots). The four branches were fitted together and checked by the full suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`).

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 37):** the callings' remaining lopsided numbers (the warrior at level 10, the mage at 30 and 50, the archer at 50, the priest played simply; see Balance of the callings), measured again on a quiet machine; the lift-off and touchdown of short-legged beasts at a walk or trot; feet stepping across under a body shoved sideways; stone towers that neither breach nor burn; arrows in flight that pass through walls.
