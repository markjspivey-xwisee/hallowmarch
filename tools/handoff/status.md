# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-13:** Version 16 is merged on `main` and published. The March is heard as it is seen: every sound is placed where it happens (faded with distance, panned, dulled by walls, doors, roofs and hills, muffled under water, the loudest few played at once), the systems that were silent have sounds made in code (masons, builders, falling houses, burning walls, fire crews, stormers, engines, oars, footsteps on what is underfoot), the places have their own air (wind, leaves, insects, rain on the roof over you, a town's murmur, the Barrow's echo, a battle heard from afar), and what matters is heard first (a shot at you, a flare, a ward breaking, your keep's bell). The March on a phone holds about a third less memory (static geometry lets go of its CPU copy once on the GPU, wrecks are made when first shown), boots sooner and makes less garbage a step, with the world byte for byte the same. Bodies move like living things in what versions 14 and 15 added (birds on real legs, a gathered gallop, a heel that rises, a mallet that meets the wall, feet on a fallen tower's stones, landings in a crouch). The late game goes on past 40: the farthest borderland camps rise with you under named risen champions, veterancy past the cap brings boons, deeds earn names worn after yours, and a veteran can plant the standard of its name. The whole suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`, two runs of `tools/campaign_soak.py 60 low`: one campaign won by hallows after 40 minutes, one by renown at the hour) was run on the merged build.

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 62):** campaigns still end by renown at the hour about half the time (keeps change hands so often that no realm holds four for long), and a band leader once stood in an enemy keep's gate passage through its change of hands; the callings' simple bots at 10 and the simple warrior's pairings at 30 and 50, the priest's mastery; no deeper descent of the Barrow and nothing a veteran can see on itself; holy ground's disc in the grass, a house's scaffold of one lift; How to play and the War page still long on a phone; the war's worst steps (a band leader's way round, a posted guard's think); a few checks that fail now and then (`music.py`'s fight's end, `loot.py`'s level 35 ratio, `water.py`'s float height, `siege3.py`'s crew scenes).
