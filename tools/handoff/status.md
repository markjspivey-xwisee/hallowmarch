# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-11:** Version 12 is merged on `main` and published. It looks at the whole world as a player sees it and mends what it found (`look.py`'s gallery: trodden greens and footpaths, house walls without light lines at their openings, thatch combed one way, foliage that parts by cards and clumps, robes over striding legs, bodies lit by the room they stand in); measures and holds every gait (feet raised and set down in place, the walk's four beats, the trot's diagonal pairs, the gallop's gathered leap); stops what flies at what it hits (arrows stick in timber and glance off stone, shots go out through crenels, slits and open doors) and lets stone towers breach and be mended; makes Charge rush at last and tunes the callings round it; and keeps the war from standing still (a chase, a breach, an engine or a band that gets nowhere gives up and takes new orders). The four branches were fitted together and checked by the full suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`).

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 42):** the callings' remaining gaps (the warrior against a bow and the priest at level 10, the warrior's pack survival at 30 and 50, the casters' mastery, the simple priest and archer, the archers' realm ways); the bands storming a fallen tower and the engines taking towers for their mark; Thule palisades and Ériu thatched cones that burn; houses broken by siege stones; the look's found-and-left list (footpaths from afar, roads in snow, trees near the camera at low, beasts lit indoors, the near and far trees' hand-over, a climber's weapons on a ladder); a sharp cut at a sprint that now and then lets a planted foot slide.
