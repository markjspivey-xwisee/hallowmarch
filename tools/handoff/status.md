# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-12:** Version 15 is merged on `main` and published. The war keeps moving: every band either gets on with its order or fights (`campaign_soak.py` now measures that every 2 s over whole campaigns), soldiers come down off the walls and out of the ruins, townsfolk run to their houses' fires and beat them out, and broken gates are kept with the world; fitting it together found and fixed two places a band stood for good (the strip behind a burh's hall, the inside of a rath's bank) and stormers left outside a fallen tower when their leader turned to the court. Campaigns are won by keeps or hallows in about one hour in three at low; the rest go to renown at the hour (keeps change hands so often that no realm holds four for long). The callings were measured both ways round (the side made first acts first, which decided archer mirrors in every earlier table) on open grass, the archers' realm ways became edges on their own ground, and Charge comes every 12 s. The world was looked at again (houses that fall to rubble and rise behind real scaffolds anyone can climb, scaffolds that rise lift by lift, charred timber that cracks, holy ground that glows, firefighters with beaters, the map's names laid out). Players know what to do on a phone first (the Chronicle folds to its summaries, field notes have their own page, a marker for steps done anywhere, the war panel's aim line, no tap lost to a moving panel). The whole suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`, `tools/campaign_soak.py 60 low`) was run on the merged build.

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 57):** the callings' simple bots at 10 (the priest wins 81%, the mage 24%) and the simple warrior's pairings at 30 and 50, the priest's mastery at 30 and 50; the war's brisk pace of captures (most campaigns end by renown at the hour) and a new campaign that starts with the last one's engines and breaches; a few checks that fail now and then (`siege3.py`'s crew scenes, `gaits.py`'s stair at a walk, `water.py`'s float height); the masons only in peace and not saved, a house's scaffold of one lift; How to play and the War page still long on a phone; the birds' walk, the gallop's leap, a stormer's feet on the rubble's stones, the diver's stroke.
