# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-11:** Version 13 is merged on `main` and published. The siege uses everything the walls can do: the engines batter the weaker way in (a cracked tower, or one much nearer than a curtain), bands storm a fallen tower one or two at a time over its rubble and through its store room while the garrison holds the court before its door, the burhs' palisades and Ériu's thatched cones catch, burn, fall into gaps that are stormed and are built again in peace, siege stones and meteors crack houses, hole their roofs and bring them down to rubble the townsfolk rebuild, arrows stuck in a door swing with it, and a held archer looks for another foe or a place with a line. The callings were measured on clean ground for the first time since version 9 (every table at 30 and 50 had been taken in arenas cratered by earlier meteors) and brought nearer the aim (Holy Fire flares a spell away within 10 m, holy ground dazzles, Crippling Arrow wounds while it slows, Whirlwind heals in the press, the realm ways evened out, better skilled bots). The world was looked at again (a climber's weapons slung on his back, beasts lit by the room, the near and far trees handed over by whole cards, leaves on the near crowns at low, footpaths at 1 m, trodden snow as slush, no crazy paving in a small view). Players know what to do (field notes that show on an upright phone, the first-steps note first and none over a fight, a gold gem for the next step on the minimap and the map, a path of 24 steps and the aims past it, notes, help and tips for every system since version 6). Fitting the four branches together, homing shots now go over a crater's rim or a low rise (a mage's own meteor no longer shields its mark from its bolts), and a burning palisade keeps three flames a stretch under a cap of 60 timed fires. The whole suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`) was run on the merged build.

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 47):** the callings' remaining gaps (the lopsided pairs, the priest's mastery, the mage at the band's low edge at 30 and 50, the arenas farther apart); fire on the walk's decks, a burh's gate and the timber temples; the hole in a house's roof in its shadow; engines that never aim at houses; a band smaller than about twice the garrison seldom through a tower's door; the garrison not going to a palisade fire; the look's found-and-left list (cracks that read as vines on a worn tower, the mending scaffold without boards, the two crowns' looks in the trees' hand-over band, the leaves' regular grid at low); the guide's (long Chronicle pages on a phone, toasts that wait for the first note, no gem for steps done anywhere); a sharp cut at a sprint that now and then lets a planted foot slide.
