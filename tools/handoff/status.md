# The Hallowmarch: project handoff

**Status, 2026-10-12:** Version 14 is merged on `main` and published. Fire and siege made whole: a burh's wall walks, its plank gates and the Thule hallow's log ring catch, burn, char, fall in and are built again in peace, garrisons run to a fire on their walls and beat it out (and the player can), bands find their way through the ruins, the Logres gates are passable at last (the walk over them stood solid to the ground since version 11), and a stone's hole in a roof is cut in the shadow too. The callings were measured in arenas far apart, and every skilled bot is within the band at every level (renewing by rote costs, the ward keeps a spell, a stopped Charge comes back sooner, the archers' realm ways halved). The world was looked at again (cracks that climb the joints, working scaffolds a player can climb with masons on them, trees handed over tree by tree, a hound lit by the hearth). Bodies move like living things in every action (no planted foot slides at a sharp cut or a halt, blows over still feet, climbers on the rungs, rowers on the grips). Fitting the four branches together: beating out flames over feet that stand, guards left up on a walk going down by the castle's ways, firefighters back to their own floor. The whole suite (`tools/tests/*.py`, `tools/soak.py 10`, `tools/campaign_soak.py 60 low` twice on each of versions 13 and 14) was run on the merged build.

**Open points (see the Roadmap, row 52):** the war's life over whole campaigns (keeps change hands, but the bands' stalls and the chases given up grew, and campaigns often run long or end by renown); the callings' simple bots and mastery (the simple priest and mage at 10, the simple archer at 50, the archer's and mage's mastery at 10 and the priest's at 30 and 50, the archers' realm ways); what the sieges left (townsfolk at their houses' fires, the temples' pillars and stairs, the ring's gap, a burnt gate on loading, small bands at a tower's door, a member now and then stuck by a fallen gate tower); the look's list (a house brought down to rubble, the houses' scaffolds, scaffolds rising lift by lift, the desk map's labels, a priest's holy light, the burning looks up close, the firefighters' empty hands); the guide's (the Chronicle's pages on a phone, toasts that should be notes, gems for steps done anywhere, a lost tap on the companion bar); the birds' walk and the gallop's leap.
